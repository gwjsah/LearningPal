# Landing Page & Interactive Demo Parity Audit (`LANDING_PARITY.md`)

**Phase 6 — Product Honesty & Demo Re-Alignment**  
This document audits every claim, screenshot-like visual element, threshold, and feature mention across the LearningPal landing page (`Navbar.jsx`, `Hero.jsx`, `HowItWorks.jsx`, `InteractiveDemo.jsx`, `FinalCTA.jsx`, `Footer.jsx`, and `frontend/src/data/content.js`) against the real shipped application (`SubjectDashboard.jsx`, `AttemptResult.jsx`, `TeacherClassDetail.jsx`, `ClassOverviewTab.jsx`, `ClassStudentsTab.jsx`, `ClassInterventionsTab.jsx`, `TeacherValidationLab.jsx`, `ValidationReportView.jsx`, and the Spring Boot backend).

## Audit Legend
- **`MATCHES`**: The landing page or demo matches the real shipped product behavior, data rules, and UI wording.
- **`DIFFERS`**: The prior landing page or demo misstated thresholds, wording, or workflow compared to the real product.
- **`DEMO-ONLY`**: The prior demo invented a mechanic or visual that does not exist in the real product.
- **`MISSING`**: A real product capability was omitted from the landing page and interactive demo.
